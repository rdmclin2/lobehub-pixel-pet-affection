## Model-chan characters (模型娘): fan art, non-commercial

The companions are fan characters themed on AI model vendors. **None of them is an official DeepSeek, Anthropic, xAI, Google or OpenAI image**, and the vendors' names are used only to say what each character is inspired by. Each character is a data file in `src/characters/<id>.json` (with its own credits, shown in the app under ⋯ → 素材署名) and a pet pack in `src/renderer/packs/` (format in [docs/pet-packs.md](docs/pet-packs.md)).

- **克洛蒂 (Claude 娘) and 格萝可 (Grok 娘)** — `packs/claude-pixel/`, `packs/grok-pixel/`. Identity anchors (hair, palette, outfit, motifs, theme colour) from [lmy414/ai-chan-sheets](https://github.com/lmy414/ai-chan-sheets) `设定图/11_Claude娘_Anthropic` and `设定图/09_Grok娘_xAI` (**CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**); original designs by ZipZipPipe, 《AI娘化》 ([Pixiv 148186519](https://www.pixiv.net/artworks/148186519); the author allows use with attribution, **no commercial use**). The sprite sheets were generated from text prompts with OpenAI `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst` through LobeHub (`lh generate image`) and turned into pixel art by `scripts/art/` (prompts in `scripts/art/model_chan_prompts.py`); no image from ai-chan-sheets' unlicensed `参考原图/` folder was used. Both are drawn with adult proportions; Grok's revealing details (off-shoulder dress, sheer tights, thigh band) were replaced with a high-necked gown and opaque tights, and no X logo or lettering is drawn.
- **杰米 (Gemini 娘)** — `packs/gemini-pixel/`, added 2026-10-29 the same way: identity anchors from ai-chan-sheets `设定图/10_Gemini娘` (**CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**), original design by ZipZipPipe (《AI娘化》 p04, attribution, **no commercial use**); sprite sheets generated from text prompts with `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst` (prompts `gemini_base` / `gemini_extra` in `scripts/art/model_chan_prompts.py`) and pixelated by `scripts/art/` (generated on a green key, `--key green`, since her pink-violet hair is too close to magenta). Drawn with adult proportions; the original crop top, shorts and thigh band were replaced with a high-necked top, an ankle-length skirt and opaque tights. The four-pointed star is used only as a pattern; no Google logo or lettering is drawn.
- **吉皮缇 (GPT 娘)** — `packs/gpt-pixel/`, added 2026-10-29 the same way: identity anchors from ai-chan-sheets `设定图/02_GPT娘_白龙娘` (**CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**), original design by ZipZipPipe (《AI娘化》 p03, attribution, **no commercial use**); sprite sheets generated from text prompts with `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst` (prompts `gpt_base` / `gpt_extra` in `scripts/art/model_chan_prompts.py`) and pixelated by `scripts/art/` (`slice_sheet.py --octree`, so her small props keep their colours next to the white figure). Drawn with adult proportions; the original's bare shoulders, thigh-high slit, thigh band and sheer overcoat were replaced with long sleeves, a closed ankle-length skirt and an opaque overcoat, and the wings were made small and folded. The white rosette knot is drawn as a cord ornament only; no OpenAI logo or lettering is drawn.
- **大肥鱼 (Dafeiyu, DeepSeek 娘)** — besides the dsh-pet animations above, `packs/dafeiyu-pixel/` (her default look: the same whale-girl maid redrawn from text prompts at the adult proportions of the other model-chan, after ai-chan-sheets `设定图/03_DeepSeek娘_鲸鱼娘`, not traced from the videos) depict the community character, so they keep its terms.
- **Memes (梗图).** `src/renderer/assets/memes/<character>/` holds each character's meme stickers. 大肥鱼's nine are from dsh-pet (terms above). 克洛蒂's, 格萝可's, 杰米's and 吉皮缇's (four each) were generated on 2026-10-29 with `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst` as one 2×2 sticker sheet per character from text prompts using the same identity anchors as their packs, then cut into WebP; they are **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, non-commercial** like the packs.
- **大肥鱼 · 全动态** — `packs/dafeiyu-live/` is the `dafeiyu-pixel` daily idle frame cut into layers by `scripts/art/rig_cut.py` (`art/rig/dafeiyu.json`) and animated by our own renderer (`src/renderer/rig.js`, MIT); same sources and terms as `dafeiyu-pixel` (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, non-commercial). The approach (layered meshes on a deformer tree, springs) follows the idea of Live2D-style figures such as Coopanion's; no code or art from it is used.
- **旧版像素 packs** — `packs/<id>-classic/` are the five pixel packs shipped up to v0.7.0 (83×113 frames), kept as an optional look; same sources and terms as the current packs above.
- **License of this art.** Everything in `src/renderer/packs/` except `dafeiyu-live2d/` (above) is released under **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**: attribution to ZipZipPipe (original designs), lmy414 (character sheets) and rdmclin2 (pixel art), **non-commercial only**, adaptations under the same license.
